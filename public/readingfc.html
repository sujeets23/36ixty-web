Synopsis.

Synopsis.

36ixty Booths partnered with Savoys Club for their exclusive event at The Dilly in London. Our aim was to elevate the guest experience through a dynamic blend of interactive entertainment including our Retro Booth, premium Mirror Men performers, and a professional close‑up magician. The result was a highly engaging and visually striking evening that captured attention from the moment guests arrived.

Challenges & Solutions.

Challenges & Solutions.

Challenge 1 : Managing entertainment placement within a premium, high‑footfall venue

Solution: We coordinated with The Dilly’s event team to strategically position each activation for visibility while maintaining smooth guest flow.

Challenge 2: Balancing multiple entertainment elements without disruption

Solution: A coordinated running order ensured the magician and Mirror Men engaged guests during peak social moments, while the Retro Booth served as a consistent anchor point.

Challenge 3: Maximizing visual impact for branding & social sharing

Solution: The reflective Mirror Men, Retro Booth backdrop and close‑up magic moments provided high‑quality visual content ideal for sharing across social platforms.
